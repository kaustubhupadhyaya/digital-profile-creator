title: Decisions
---
I decide with a deadline, not a feeling. Write the options, pick the one I
would defend in six months, then stop reopening it. Reversible decisions get
ten minutes. Irreversible ones get a walk and a second opinion.

Most bad decisions I have made came from being polite when I should have
been clear.
