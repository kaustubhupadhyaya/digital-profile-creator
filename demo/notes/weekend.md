title: Weekends
---
A good weekend has one plan and a lot of unclaimed time. Bread on Saturday
morning, a long walk, maybe the climbing wall if my hands agree. I need a
quiet Sunday to feel like a person on Monday. Social plans stacked back to
back make me brittle.
